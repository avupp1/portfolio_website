Hi!
I’m Avanthika Vuppala, currently pursuing Aerospace Engineering at Rutgers University. What draws me to this field is the challenge of designing systems that don’t just work in theory, but actually perform in real-world conditions where precision, efficiency, and reliability matter. I’m especially interested in how different engineering components interact and how small design decisions can significantly impact overall system performance.

My main interests include aerospace systems design, propulsion, structural analysis, advanced materials, and robotics. I’m particularly fascinated by propulsion systems and how they can be optimized for efficiency and performance. I’m also interested in advanced materials and how they can be used to create lighter, stronger, and more durable aerospace structures.

I’m also very interested in robotics and autonomous systems across aerospace, including applications in satellites, aviation, drones, and space exploration systems. I enjoy learning how robotics can improve automation, control, and reliability in complex aerospace environments.

I’m working toward a career where I can contribute to the development of innovative and reliable aerospace technologies while continuing to grow my skills through research, projects, and hands-on engineering experience.

Outside of engineering, I enjoy crocheting, painting, drawing, and trying new cuisines. I also design my own nails with 3D art, paint with oil pastels, and build small 3D structures out of origami. I’ve always been drawn to creating things, and that creativity shapes the way I approach both my hobbies and my engineering work.
